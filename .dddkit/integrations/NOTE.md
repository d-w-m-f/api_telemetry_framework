This note is context only, not part of the dddkit structure.

Both manifests are real, generated by `.dddkit/scripts/generate-manifest.py`
(`--target dddkit` / `--target claude`) and checked by `validate-ddd.py`.

`claude.manifest.json` covers all 11 SDK skills under `.claude/skills/` — the
ten pipeline skills prefixed `ddd-` and the one internal helper prefixed
`dddintern-`. `generate-manifest.py` picks them up by directory listing, so a
newly added skill is covered automatically; rerun `--target claude` after adding,
renaming, or editing any of them, or `validate-ddd.py` check 5 fails on a hash
mismatch (which is the point).

`dddkit.manifest.json` covers everything under `.dddkit/` except this directory:
the manifests cannot hash each other or themselves, since their `installed_at`
timestamp would make that self-referential and immediately stale.
